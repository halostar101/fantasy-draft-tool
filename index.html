    V2 methodology

    • VOLS: projected points above the modeled last starter at that position after league-wide FLEX demand is allocated.
    • VORP: projected points above the deeper waiver/replacement line after modeled benches are filled.
    • No double count: starter advantage and VORP are no longer simply added together; VORP mainly supplies a floor for bench/depth value.
    • ESPN prior: ESPN rank contributes 35% of base value in the same value scale, accounting for information that point projections alone may miss without forcing the model to copy ESPN.
    • Two-pick lookahead: for every candidate, the model estimates the best value likely to survive to your following pick across all positions and scores the combined path.
    • Wait / Gone: same-position wait cost and directional disappearance probability still use ESPN rank because the source does not include ADP distributions.
